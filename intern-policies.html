Intern Policies

A plain-language reference for check-ins, credits, mentor relationships, evidence, delays, suspensions, and Demo Day gates.

Core idea

HighBar protects interns by making expectations explicit, and protects employers by keeping a passed HighBar credential meaningful.

Monthly check-ins

  • Interns progress through four monthly check-ins before Demo Day can be greenlit.
  • Each check-in costs $100 or one qualified check-in credit.
  • Credits are spent when the check-in is scheduled.
  • A failed check-in does not erase progress, but every extra check-in still costs $100 or one credit.

Project rigor standard

  • HighBar does not use hobby, business, moonshot, or other lighter/heavier project tiers.
  • The project rigor level is always enough to support a post-high-school full-time job in a technically rigorous industry.
  • Small projects can qualify, but only if the evidence shows real technical judgment, working execution, and the ability to explain trade-offs under review.
  • Projects that are interesting but not yet job-standard should keep building before monthly check-ins or Demo Day represent them as HighBar-ready.

Demo Day gate

  • Demo Day scheduling unlocks only after the intern passes monthly check-in #4.
  • Demo Day is a larger check-in with more pressure, more observers, and a final pass/fail decision.
  • The pass/fail cutoff is 75% across the six review signals.
  • Failed Demo Days should include repair instructions and a path to a later attempt.

Credits

  • Mentors receive 2 monthly check-in credits to donate to 2 separate interns.
  • Board members have the same mentor credit access.
  • Every intern receives 1 monthly credit they can give to another intern.
  • Credits lower cost; they do not guarantee a pass.
  • If a delay exceeds 30 days, the consequence is minus one check-in credit.

Interns becoming mentors

  • An intern can request mentor status after passing 2 monthly check-ins.
  • Once approved as a mentor, that intern receives 2 monthly mentor credits to donate.
  • This is meant to make progress contagious: interns who are learning can still help someone just behind them.
  • Mentor status can be paused if the intern is suspended or falls outside cadence boundaries.

Mentor relationships

  • Intern/mentor relationships expire if the intern is more than 30 days late for a required check-in.
  • Relationships expire immediately if the intern is suspended for evidence, conduct, or cadence reasons.
  • Mentors may report missed meetings, counterfeit evidence concerns, or communication breakdowns from their dashboard.
  • Expired relationships can restart later if the intern returns to good standing.

Evidence video

  • For each monthly check-in or Demo Day review, HighBar accepts exactly one official evidence, presentation, and artifact format: a single public YouTube video.
  • The video must be 30-60 seconds long and posted within 24 hours before the check-in or Demo Day.
  • The intern's voice should walk reviewers through the work inside that one video.
  • Webcam should be on for 100% of the video, plus screen sharing and live footage of the work.
  • Do not submit a second video, screenshot packet, slide deck, private link, or long write-up as the review artifact.
  • Interns should name outside help, copied code, AI assistance, prototypes, simulations, and unfinished parts clearly.

Failure walls

  • After 3 failed check-ins, the intern must wait 6 months before the next check-in.
  • After 6 failed check-ins, the intern must wait 1 year before another check-in.
  • These walls protect interns from spending before evidence is ready, protect reviewers from repeated low-signal meetings, and protect the HighBar credential.

Suspension reasons

  • Counterfeit evidence or misrepresented authorship.
  • Repeated missed check-ins without communication.
  • Harassment, abusive behavior, or unsafe conduct toward mentors, board members, interns, or staff.
  • Repeatedly scheduling meetings without prepared public evidence.
  • Any other conduct that makes fair review impossible.
