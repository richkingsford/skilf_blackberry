Interns

A Skilf intern is anyone earning a Skilf through real tech project evidence, with a clear mission: help you get your first real tech job offer. There are no lighter project tiers: the rigor level is always enough to support a post-high-school full-time job in a technically rigorous industry, and each review accepts one public YouTube video between 30 and 60 seconds.
