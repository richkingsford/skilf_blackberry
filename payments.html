Payment infrastructure

Pay for check-ins, Demo Day, or sponsorships

Skilf should use Stripe Checkout for paid check-ins and Demo Day fees, with separate donation flows for tax-deductible gifts once nonprofit status and Stripe nonprofit pricing are approved.

Recommended setup

Stripe Checkout is low-maintenance, works well with Netlify Functions, and keeps payment data out of our codebase.
