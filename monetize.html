Monetize Your Expertise

Turn your hard-won knowledge into a premium revenue stream through Skilf defense days.

Every Skilf defense day pairs a candidate with an expert review panel. As a panelist, you earn a fee each time you serve. The more credibility you build, the higher your rate climbs.

How the fee ladder works

Your defense-day fee is tied to three things: the number of defenses you have completed, the quality ratings candidates give you, and the scarcity of your domain. Move up the ladder by doing great work consistently.

Apprentice Panelist

0–4 completed defenses. You are building your track record and learning the format.

$150 / session
Journeyman Panelist

5–14 defenses with an average rating above 4.2 / 5. Candidates start seeking you out.

$350 / session
Master Panelist

15+ defenses, average rating above 4.6, and at least one scarce-domain badge.

$750 / session
Grand Master

30+ defenses, 4.8+ rating, recognized thought leader. Invited to design new defense formats.

$1,500 / session

Boost your rate faster

  • Specialize in a scarce domain. Panels in quantum hardware, computational biology, and climate informatics command a premium because fewer experts qualify.
  • Write a post-defense debrief. Candidates who receive a written summary rate their panelists significantly higher, which accelerates your climb.
  • Mentor a candidate before their defense. Pre-defense mentoring sessions are billed separately and signal to the platform that you invest in outcomes.
  • Maintain a streak. Completing five consecutive defenses without a cancellation unlocks an automatic 10% rate bump at your current tier.
  • Cross-pollinate. Serving on panels outside your primary domain (with approval) earns a versatility badge that unlocks priority scheduling.

Getting paid

Fees are deposited within 48 hours of a completed defense. You choose your payout method during onboarding — direct deposit, PayPal, or Wise. There are no platform deductions at the Apprentice and Journeyman tiers. At Master and above, Skilf retains a 10% platform fee to fund candidate scholarships.
